Audio bufferHow much sound Mixxx makes at a time: smaller answers sooner, but breaks up if the device can't keep up. Optimize tests this device under a heavy load (silently, decks empty) and keeps the smallest that doesn't break up
Key notationHow the deck shows its current key (after key shift, and tempo when key lock is off)
FX layoutBeat FX: one effect per side with its beat length, LEVEL and ON, like Pioneer's. Multi-FX: three effects at once, a knob each (tap it: on/off; SHIFT + tap: next effect) and MIX, like Serato's
Live stemsWhen a deck loads a track without stems, the phone separates it (about half a minute) and swaps in the stems version; STEM pads show how far it is
Controller modeControl Mixxx on a computer instead: over the USB cable (set it to MIDI in Android's USB notification, then pick the ZyDeck mapping for it in Mixxx), or over Wi-Fi with ZyDeck Link running on the computer
MIDI layoutZyDeck: Mixxx with the ZyDeck mapping (everything). Standard: plain notes and CCs for MIDI Learn in Serato, rekordbox or Traktor (no waveforms or positions come back)
DiscordPlays the mix in a Discord voice channel through the ZyDeck Discord bot (on the computer: launch.sh; in Discord: /zydeck join from the voice channel). Listeners hear it about a second behind
Sampler kitsLoad a set of pads (with their modes) or save what's on them now

Default behaviour on assignment: keylockThe sample keeps its pitch when its tempo changes. SHIFT + a loaded pad changes it for that pad
