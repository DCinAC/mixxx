          Controller mode

          Turns this phone into a MIDI controller for Mixxx on a computer. Over the USB cable: choose MIDI in Android's USB notification, then in Mixxx open Preferences › Controllers, pick the phone and load the ZyDeck mapping. With ZyDeck Link running on the computer, ZyDeck offers to link over Wi-Fi, so you can unplug the cable. Track names and waveforms need ZyDeck Link too.

          Paste cookies instead
          You log in on Bilibili's own page; Zydek keeps only the session cookie, privately on this phone, and uses it for downloads (no more blocks, and Hi-Res audio with premium). Sessions run out after a while: log in again then. Dolby audio is skipped: Mixxx here can't decode it.

          Library

          Default behaviour on assignment

          What a pad gets when a track, a capture or a cut sample is put on it. Each pad can change both afterwards: SHIFT + the pad on the decks.

          Sampler kits

          A kit remembers what's on the 32 sampler pads, with each pad's mode (one-shot, hold, loop) and tempo setting. Loading one fills the pads, and empties the ones that were empty in it.

          Stem server (PC)

          Tap a track and choose "Make stems on the PC". A PC running Zydek's stem server splits tracks into vocals, drums, bass and other; they come back as .stem.m4a files in Music/Zydek Web. Type its address and port (8770 if you leave it out).

          Audio output

          …
          ZyDeck measures how late the sound comes out of the current output (Bluetooth adds a lot) and draws the waveforms that much later, so they match what you hear. Fine-tune with the slider if the beats still look early or late.
          "Android: follow the system" plays wherever Android sends music: Bluetooth or USB when connected, the speaker otherwise. Bluetooth adds a lot of delay: use wired or USB audio to mix.
          Android plays to one device at a time, so main and headphones go on one device's channels: a USB interface with 4 outputs (main 1–2, headphones 3–4), or split one pair of earphones (main on 1 left, headphones on 2 right).
